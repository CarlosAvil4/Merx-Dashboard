# Dashboard-Company Merx

### Dashboard Link : https://app.powerbi.com/groups/me/reports/eab8318f-9c66-43cb-a9fe-27e75cf0eae3/ReportSection

## Problem Statement

The Merx dashboard is an analytical solution designed for a large-scale supermarket, providing a comprehensive view of key sales and profitability metrics across the year’s four-month term. With data available from 2013 to 2020, the dashboard allows filtering by year, category, subcategory, and product tags. The main categories include Food & Beverages, Household Items, and Health & Beauty, with subcategories such as beverages, deli, personal care, wines & liquors, and more.

On the first page, users can analyze a sales percentage chart by category, a comparison of physical vs. online purchases, and a detailed table showing total sales, total profit, and percentage margin by subcategory and product tag. The second page presents a metric comparison, featuring a bar chart for total sales with an overlaid line graph for percentage margin, as well as a comparative analysis of online vs. physical purchases and a historical monthly and yearly metrics table. Finally, the last page includes a dynamic table where data can be viewed at the product, brand, or tag level, with a search bar enabling quick product lookup.


### Steps Followed for Creating SkuDon's Dashboard

- Step 1 : Load the dataset into Power BI Desktop. The dataset contains sales and profit data for a supermarket from 2013 to 2020.

- Step 2: Power Query Editor: Enabled "Column distribution," "Column quality," and "Column profile" options.

- Step 3: Data Validation; Checked for null values and errors, finding no significant issues.

- Step 4: Created new measures to calculate total sales, total profit, and profit percentage.

- Step 5: Designed the first report page:

  (a) Added a category sales percentage chart to compare Food & Beverages, Household Items, and Health & Beauty

  (b) Created a pie chart comparing physical vs. online purchases

  (c) Inserted a table showing totals for sales, profit, and margin by subcategory and product tag

- Step 6: Designed the second report page for metric comparisons

  (d) Added a bar chart comparing total sales per category

  (e) Overlayed a line graph displaying the profit margin percentage across the same categories

  (f) Included a comparative chart for online vs. physical purchases over time

  (g) Inserted a historical metrics table, displaying sales, profit, and margin by month and year.

- Step 7: Created the final report page:
Added a dynamic table with sales, profit, and margin per product, allowing users to drill down by product, brand, or tag.
Included a search bar for quick product lookup.

- Step 8: Applied filters for enhanced user interaction:
Filters were added for year, category, subcategory, and product tags.

- Step 9: Applied formatting and themes:
Adjusted colors and fonts for a professional and visually appealing layout.
Used consistent data labels and legends for clarity.

- Step 10: Published the report to Power BI Service for easy access and collaboration.
